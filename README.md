# Handicap-XR
Handicap XR is an XR app developed in SwiftUI for Apple’s Vision Pro. It aims to raise awareness about various disabilities by simulating their effects in immersive environments.
This project was developed by a team of four students as part of our academic curriculum.
The app includes three distinct disability simulations, which can be experienced individually or as part of a predefined sequence. Each simulation allows users to engage in everyday activities to better understand the challenges faced by individuals with these conditions.

## Technologies
The app was developed using the Xcode IDE and the SwiftUI framework. 
Simulation assets were created using Reality Composer Pro.


## Components
### Glaucoma simulation
Glaucoma is an eye condition that affects the optic nerve and can lead to vision loss.
In this simulation, the user’s peripheral vision is darkened to simulate the restricted field of view caused by the condition. This makes tasks such as navigating through spaces or estimating distances more challenging. Activities like driving or cycling are discouraged.

### Age-Related Macular Degeneration simulation
ARMD affects the central vision, making it harder to see right in front of you.
During the simulation, the center of the user’s vision is obscured. This makes tasks like reading, pouring a drink, or picking up small objects more difficult.

### Schizophrenia Simulation
Simulating this condition in a VR environment is challenging, as experiences vary widely among individuals. Schizophrenia includes a range of symptoms such as hallucinations or disorganized thinking.
In our simulation, users experience auditory hallucinations through randomly triggered, high-volume sounds, and visual disturbances like shifting shadows. Holding a conversation or staying focused during these interruptions is intentionally difficult.
